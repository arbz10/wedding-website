How it all began

Our Love Story

  1. Spring 2019

    First Meeting

    A rainy afternoon, one umbrella and a café with only one free table. We shared it — and talked until closing.

  2. Summer 2020

    First Date

    A picnic by the river that turned into a sunset, then into stargazing. Neither of us wanted to go home.

  3. Winter 2025

    The Proposal

    Back at that same little café, a ring hidden in a slice of lemon cake. She said yes before he finished asking.

  4. June 2027

    Forever Begins

    And now we can't wait to say “I do” surrounded by the people we love most — you.
